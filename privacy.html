Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

This policy covers the Rap Sheet Replay website and Rap Sheet Replay Publisher, our own tool for posting our clips. Questions: rapsheetreplay@gmail.com.

Who uses Rap Sheet Replay Publisher

Only us. The Publisher signs in to the Rap Sheet Replay YouTube and TikTok accounts that we own, and posts our own clips to them. It is not offered to the public and it does not collect information from other people.

What the Publisher accesses

  • YouTube (Google): the ability to upload videos to our channel, and read-only access to our channel's basic details (for example its name) to confirm we are posting to the right channel.
  • TikTok: our account's basic profile information (for example its display name), and the ability to post videos to our account.

We do not access anyone else's account, contacts, messages or viewing history.

How we use and store it

  • The sign-in tokens from Google and TikTok are stored only on our own computer and are used only to post our clips and confirm which account we are posting to.
  • We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We keep the tokens only while we use the Publisher. Deleting them from our computer, or revoking access, ends the Publisher's access.

YouTube API Services

Rap Sheet Replay Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

Rap Sheet Replay Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TikTok

TikTok's handling of data is covered by the TikTok Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions.

This website

This site has no sign-up, no forms, no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Rap Sheet Replay is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Email rapsheetreplay@gmail.com with any question or deletion request.
